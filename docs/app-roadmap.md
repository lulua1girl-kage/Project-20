## Completion rule

An app is marked complete only after its scope is implemented, its documented checks pass, and its build has been verified.

## Ecosystem direction

Project-20 apps remain independently installable. A shared Nori Account broker and permission-scoped integration layer will be designed separately; no app may claim account connectivity until the broker, signing model, authorization, and data-sharing contract are implemented and tested.
